Honest limits
What it isn't.

    

      
The checkpointer is in memory
A restart forgets a suspended plan. A Postgres checkpointer is the next step.

      
The gateway is a simulator
Commands are pulled and applied by the device simulator. There is no building management system on the other end.

      
It proposes; it doesn't optimise
Nothing decides what the setpoint should be. That needs the energy model validated against a measured building first — and it is analytically verified, not validated.
